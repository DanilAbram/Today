# Today

Inferred from the approved design (docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-11-today-website-design.md); assumptions marked.

- **What:** a one-page website for planning a single day: a few tasks, small habits, a 25-minute focus timer, a streak.
- **Who:** the owner, at the start of the day and in short check-ins (assumption: student / personal use, phone + laptop).
- **Mode:** Operate. Calm, fast, nothing to learn.
- **Feel:** a warm paper notebook in the morning light. Quiet, confident, a little literary. Not a productivity dashboard.
- **Anti-references:** gamified dashboards, KPI cards, neon gradients, nagging.
- **Theme:** light by default (daytime use), follows system dark mode, manual toggle.
